Today and Achievement detail

Light and dark, with Lena’s example data on Thursday, 05.11.2026, 18:40 (Swiss formats). The frames are live: tap a check circle, Show on the private row, Path / List, or ⋯ → Pause…. Numbered pins match the developer notes next to each board. The toolbar switches all frames to 150 % / 200 % text, Android, reduced motion or full length.

Today

One job: check in. Level and XP on top, then today’s quests from all achievements with a one-tap check-in, the active achievements as rings, and the running streaks. “Lose 10 kg” is private, so its quests collapse into a single row until Lena taps Show.

Achievement detail

The quest line itself: why it matters, overall progress, the next quest as the one primary action, and the vertical quest path — walked part solid, the way ahead dashed, locked quests with a lock and what unlocks them, the bonus quest on a side branch. The list view shows the same quests grouped by state.

Interaction states

The check-in sheet for a target quest (the value is shown here although overview screens hide it), the moment right after a one-tap check-in (+10 XP, undo snackbar) and the pause confirmation, which states the consequences plainly and gives both choices the same weight.

200 % text and Android

At accessibility text sizes the layout reflows instead of truncating: rings become a list, quest cards drop their lead icon, the level card stacks. Android keeps the same design with its own system bars and back arrow.
