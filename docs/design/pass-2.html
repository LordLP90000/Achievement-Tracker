Every other screen and state

Built on the approved direction and your answers (no Archive, pause starts over, ranks shown, gambles opt-in, generic notifications by default, reminder times and hide-numbers synced). Frames show their full length; sheets and dialogs show in a phone viewport. The clickable main flow is in the prototype.

Onboarding

Three screens at most: how it works (achievement → quests → XP), the privacy promise, and the first goal in the user’s own words.

Sign up and sign in

Email and password to sign up, passkey first to sign in, the “check your email” state and the two-step code.

Consent

The explicit, unticked consent for goal content, its plain-language summary, and one calm passkey offer.

Today: states

First launch, nothing due, loading, offline and error. Today now separates Today’s quests (due habits) from Up next (undated quests, whenever you’re ready) — see the pass 2 notes.

Achievements

The list with its status filter and row actions (iOS swipe, Android long-press), and the detail screen when paused, private behind App lock, and completed (read-only, with badge and rank).

Create achievement

Four steps, two of them skippable: goal and category (or a template), why and when, quests, privacy and reminders.

Add and edit quests

One bottom sheet with type-specific fields: schedule for habits, start and target values for targets, size, prerequisite, bonus and reminder.

Quest detail

Check-in history, streak calendar for habits, progress chart for targets, notes — and a locked quest.

Celebrations

Small for a quest, full screen for an unlocked achievement (with and without motion), medium for a level up, and the share card where the user picks what’s shown.

Stats and trophy room

XP over time, totals, best streaks and badges; the trophy room explains ranks in plain words.

XP gambles (opt-in)

Off by default. Turning them on, placing one, the running state and a neutral end.

Profile and settings

Account, security (password, passkeys, two-step, devices), notifications, appearance and App lock.

Privacy & data

What is stored and why, the consent, download, and account deletion with re-authentication and a final confirmation — without guilt.

Around the app

What leaves the app: notifications on the Lock Screen, the app-switcher cover, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets — none of them ever shows a private achievement.
